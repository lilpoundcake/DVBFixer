bonded neighbor in canonical protein context. Observed/kept H are never rewritten.

## Complete graph-H control: selected-phase result does not generalize

The fixed-zero arm prevents this particular inward endpoint; the much lower
initial-energy selected arm does not. Thus the earlier selected-arm H-stage
benefit on 1SHG is **not a general fix or reliable geometry predictor**. Conversely,
this is not evidence that fixed-zero is safe: previous 1SHG whole-system
near-planarity/negative-Cα regressions remain, and the current fixed-zero 1TIM
endpoints retain severe H strain elsewhere.

Across the ten contexts, median maximum final H-bond deviations are
**0.04139 / 0.04679 / 0.04013 Å** (unchanged/zero/selected); maximum H-angle
deviations are **71.533 / 72.281 / 62.324°**. In 1TIM seed 1, maximum H-bond
deviation is **0.15132 / 0.22815 / 0.17097 Å**. None of these arms is complete
H-geometry acceptance. Even selected 1TIM seed 2 has a higher final H-System
energy than unchanged (49335.169 vs 48090.187 kJ/mol), despite lower initial
energy. Reported <1 Å nonbonded H-contact counts (excluding direct bonds/common
neighbors) are geometric descriptors, not a new favorable acceptance threshold.

This study moves no heavy atoms, so it cannot establish downstream Cα flattening
or its prevention. All **10 original H-to-whole starts** separately reproduce
exactly in nm using native-label repair and object-index mapping. A separately
frozen all-context downstream study is underway: independent saved-State native
baselines precede all30 continuations, with original Reference/no-solvent/two
1000-iteration phases and tenfold restraint reduction. No new phases/seeds or
force/exclusion tuning are permitted. Its results are not inferred from this
